HELP

Keep it simple

Flow tab not found?

Open flow.google.com in the active window, sign in, and keep that tab visible while a queue runs.

Prompt box not found?

Flow changes its interface occasionally. Refresh the Flow tab and reload this extension from chrome://extensions.

Where are files saved?

Completed results are sent to your Downloads/FlowBridge folder. No result is uploaded by this extension.
